# Product Overview

ChemActiva Innovations is a sustainable nano cellulose company focused on environmental solutions through advanced chemical processes and green technologies.

## Core Business
- **Primary Product**: Cellu® - high-performance, biodegradable cellulose nanocrystals
- **Product Forms**: Powder and liquid variants
- **Target Markets**: Domestic and marine oil spill management, sustainable materials

## Key Products
- Domestic Oil Spill Kits - safe solutions for home kitchens
- Marine Oil Spill Kits - biodegradable marine cleanup solutions
- Cellulose Nanocrystals - renewable plant-based materials

## Company Focus Areas
- Sustainable chemicals and eco-friendly materials
- Plant-based products replacing petroleum-based materials
- Bio-based products from renewable biological resources
- Smart materials with self-healing and adaptive properties

## Mission & Vision
- **Mission**: Innovate cutting-edge chemical technologies for a cleaner, sustainable planet
- **Vision**: Global leader in clean technology driving sustainability and environmental stewardship

## Location
Based at RISE Foundation IISER, IISER Kolkata, West Bengal, India
